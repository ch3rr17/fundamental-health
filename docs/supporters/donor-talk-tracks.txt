* Target audience: who this track is built for

TRACK 01  |  COMMUNITY EMOTIONAL CONNECTION
Neighbors in Need: The Community Story
TARGET AUDIENCE
San Diego residents with demonstrated philanthropic giving, mental health affinity, $100K+ income
HOOK
Mental health struggles don't happen in institutions. They happen next door.
BODY
In San Diego, 1 in 5 adults is living with a mental health condition. That's your neighbor, your coworker, the person you see at the farmers market on Saturday mornings. Many of them can't afford care. Many of them don't know where to turn.
FundaMental Health is a San Diego nonprofit changing that. Through our Foundation of Care program, we cover the cost of therapy for neighbors who can't afford it: youth in crisis, veterans carrying invisible wounds, elders facing grief and isolation.
We believe your zip code shouldn't determine whether you get the support you need. And we believe San Diegans who care about their community can help change that.
CALL TO ACTION
We'd love to share what this work looks like up close. Would you be open to a 20-minute conversation about what FundaMental Health is building in our city?
APOLLO USAGE NOTE
Use this track for lists filtered by: San Diego location, mental health cause affinity, philanthropic behavior signals. Apollo variable insertions: first name, employer/industry if available.
	







TRACK 02  |  CASE STUDY INTEREST
Neighbors in Need: The Case Study
TARGET AUDIENCE
Marketing professionals, nonprofit strategists, foundation program officers, mission-driven business leaders
HOOK
We built a donor campaign around three fictional neighbors. It turned into something much bigger.
BODY
FundaMental Health recently developed Neighbors in Need, a donor storytelling campaign that introduced three composite personas drawn from the real people we serve: Alex, an 18-year-old navigating housing instability after coming out. Victor, a 42-year-old veteran carrying PTSD and pride in equal measure. Rosa, a 70-year-old Latina widow processing grief in a culture that doesn't have easy language for it.
The campaign was designed to make invisible needs visible. We documented the entire process, from clinical review to illustration to donor activation, and turned it into a case study for other nonprofits.
The result is available as a 1-page summary or a full-length case study with behind-the-scenes storytelling, creative process breakdowns, and campaign results.
CALL TO ACTION
I'd love to send you a copy. Which version would be more useful to you right now: the 1-pager or the full case study?
APOLLO USAGE NOTE
Use this track for: nonprofit leaders, foundation staff, marketing/communications professionals, social sector consultants. Frame as a peer-to-peer knowledge share, not a donation ask. Works well for warm list building.
	





TRACK 03  |  BOARD RECRUITMENT
Leadership Opportunity: FundaMental Health Board
TARGET AUDIENCE
Senior professionals, attorneys, financial advisors, healthcare executives, civic leaders
HOOK
We're building a board that matches the ambition of our mission.
BODY
FundaMental Health is a San Diego behavioral health nonprofit at an inflection point. We operate two programs: Foundation of Care, which provides donation-funded therapy to underserved neighbors, and Bridge to Care, our Medi-Cal Enhanced Care Management model serving City Heights and surrounding communities.
We're growing. And we're looking for leaders who want to shape what that growth looks like.
Board membership at FundaMental Health is an active partnership. We're looking for people with expertise in areas like finance, law, healthcare, communications, fundraising, and community development. People who want to do more than attend quarterly meetings. People who believe access to mental health care is one of the defining equity issues of our time.
If that resonates, we'd love to have a conversation about whether there's a fit.
CALL TO ACTION
Would you be open to a brief call to learn more about what board membership looks like at FundaMental Health? No commitment required.
APOLLO USAGE NOTE
Use this track for: senior professionals with nonprofit board history, attorneys/CPAs, healthcare executives, civic-minded business owners. Prioritize contacts with existing governance or advisory experience in LinkedIn/Apollo data.
	

TRACK 04  |  FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS & CRA
Community Investment Alignment: CRA and Behavioral Health
TARGET AUDIENCE
Community bank executives, credit union leaders, CRA officers, regional bank VP-level contacts
HOOK
Mental health access is a Community Reinvestment Act opportunity most banks haven't noticed yet.
BODY
Financial institutions under CRA obligations increasingly need to demonstrate investment in underserved communities, particularly in areas like health, housing stability, and workforce readiness. Mental health is at the intersection of all three.
FundaMental Health operates two programs in San Diego: direct therapy access for low-income and unhoused individuals through Foundation of Care, and Enhanced Care Management for Medi-Cal patients in City Heights through Bridge to Care. Both programs serve populations and geographies that map directly to CRA assessment areas.
We're actively seeking community investment partners who see behavioral healthcare not just as a charitable cause, but as a strategic alignment with their CRA goals and community impact commitments.
CALL TO ACTION
I'd welcome the chance to share how other financial institutions have structured partnerships with behavioral health nonprofits for CRA credit. Would a 20-minute call be worth your time?
APOLLO USAGE NOTE
Use this track for: CRA officers, community bank presidents/VPs, credit union executives in San Diego County. Reference City Heights geography specifically when Apollo data shows bank branches or assessment area overlap.
	

TRACK 05  |  DAF MANAGERS & GIVING CIRCLES
Funding Mental Health Access: A Case for Giving Circles and DAF Advisors
TARGET AUDIENCE
Donor-advised fund advisors, giving circle organizers, women's philanthropy networks, community foundation program staff
HOOK
Behavioral health is one of the most underfunded causes relative to demonstrated community need.
BODY
For DAF holders and giving circle members looking to deploy capital with measurable impact, mental health access represents a compelling opportunity. Nationally, mental health receives just 7% of healthcare philanthropic dollars despite accounting for a disproportionate share of emergency room visits, homelessness, and workforce disruption.
FundaMental Health offers a direct, transparent model. Donations to Foundation of Care cover the actual cost of therapy sessions for neighbors who cannot afford care. Bridge to Care extends our reach through the Medi-Cal system. We can show you exactly how dollars translate to sessions, and sessions translate to outcomes.
We also offer giving circle programming: briefings, site visits, and impact reports designed to engage members who want to connect personally with the work they're funding.
CALL TO ACTION
If you're advising donors who care about health equity, or leading a giving circle looking for a high-impact local cause, I'd love to make a direct introduction to our team. Would that be a fit for anyone in your current portfolio?
APOLLO USAGE NOTE
Use this track for: Silicon Valley Community Foundation advisors, San Diego Foundation program staff, women's philanthropy networks (Women Give San Diego, etc.), religious-affiliated giving circles. Lead with the DAF angle if Apollo data includes 'philanthropic advisor' or 'wealth management' signals.
