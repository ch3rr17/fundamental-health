# Track 01 — Community Donors (Mental Health Affinity)

## Limitation note

Open web search cannot verify private individuals' income or personal giving capacity ($100K+ income, "demonstrated philanthropic giving") — that data lives in Apollo's own enrichment/filters, not the public web. Attempting to name-match against income data would mean guessing, which the org's own AI Behavior Spec explicitly prohibits ("prefer omission over speculation").

What open research CAN verify: real, named individuals who have **already made public mental-health-related gifts or hold public philanthropic leadership roles** in San Diego, via press coverage and foundation announcements. This yields a short, high-confidence list (5), not 30-50 — quality over quantity, as instructed.

Below that is a set of **proxy channels** — recurring campaigns, events, and directories where this donor profile self-selects — which is the more realistic sourcing path for Apollo list-building at volume (combine with Apollo's own income/location/affinity filters).
